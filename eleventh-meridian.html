THE IDEA

Behind
the work.

The Eleventh Meridian is my independent creative house for imagining how a space can feel like its own little world. The work begins before architecture or interior execution: narrative, visual research, atmosphere, palette, guest experience, and the creative references that give a concept its identity. It is an evolving practice and personal creative venture—not a claim of completed commissioned buildings.

✳Moodboards and visual research
✳Spatial and atmospheric direction
✳Guest-experience touchpoints
✳Palette and material references
